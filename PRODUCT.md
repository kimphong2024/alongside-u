# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adult children, spouses and siblings in Singapore caring for a loved one with late-stage cancer — most of them first-time caregivers. They arrive tired, often at night, on a phone, between hospital visits and work. Cognitive load is the enemy: every screen must answer "what needs me now?" without demanding anything.

## Product Purpose

Alongside is "the keeper of time": it reduces the cognitive, logistical and emotional load of first-time caregiving. Care Orientation (a prioritized roadmap, doctor-visit records with AI notes, trusted local supports) plus Living Moments (a family scrapbook of what matters). Success = a caregiver feels less lost after five minutes in the app, and the family stays on the same page without a 2 a.m. tab spiral.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, warm, steady. A calm companion, not a tool demanding engagement — paper, not glass. Serif headings with generous whitespace; handwriting only for memory-keeping. The app never rushes, never gamifies, never guilt-trips. Warmth is carried by type, texture and copy, not by decoration.

## Anti-references

- Hospital/clinical SaaS: no sterile blue-white EMR look, no KPI-card dashboard energy, no urgency badges or red alerts. It must never feel like a hospital portal.
- No productivity-app streaks, progress pressure, or notification nagging — caregiving is not inbox-zero.
- No wellness-app gradient-pastel genericism.

## Design Principles

1. **Reduce the load** — every screen answers "what needs me now?" before anything else; fewer choices, gently ordered.
2. **Calm is functional** — softness (paper texture, muted sage/clay, slow easing) is there to lower stress, not to decorate; it must never cost legibility.
3. **The family, not the user** — copy and structure speak about "the family" and the loved one by name; personalization from onboarding is used everywhere it helps.
4. **Honest surfaces** — nothing fake: no dead links, no aspirational features dressed as real ones; mocks are labeled in demos.
5. **Heavy days come first** — the interface adapts to the caregiver's stated mood; on heavy days it offers less, softer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first (320px up), older secondary users possible (shared family account). Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast on the cream surfaces — the muted-brown text colors must be checked, not assumed. Touch targets ≥44px. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on all framer-motion choreography. Singlish/multilingual family context: plain English copy, no idioms that don't travel.
